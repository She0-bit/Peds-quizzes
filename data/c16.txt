Which primitive reflex is present at birth and disappears by 6 months?
* Moro reflex
- Palmar grasp reflex
- Tonic neck reflex
- Rooting reflex
> Official key: Moro. Moro disappears by about 4-6 months. Timings for the other reflexes vary by source, so learn Moro for this question.

* Activated charcoal
- Gastric lavage
- Intravenous fluids
- Sodium bicarbonate
> Official key: activated charcoal (salicylates are well adsorbed, and charcoal is useful even several hours after ingestion). Protect the airway first in a lethargic child. Bicarbonate for urine alkalinization follows.

A girl with type 1 diabetes has 3 days of fever, diarrhea and vomiting. She looks well hydrated, fasting glucose is 260 mg/dL, bicarbonate is normal and urine shows ketones. After adjusting the insulin dose, what is the next step?
* Admit immediately
- Tell her to measure urine ketones daily
- Tell her to measure blood glucose daily
- Tell her to increase her hydration
> Official key: admission. Ongoing vomiting with ketosis in T1DM risks rapid progression to DKA, and she cannot keep up oral intake. Sick-day monitoring at home is every 2-4 hours, not daily.
