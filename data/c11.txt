* Observe
- Steroids
- Immunoglobulin

> Newly diagnosed ITP with only skin bleeding: observation (ASH 2019), regardless of platelet count. Steroids or IVIG for mucosal/significant bleeding. Check what your lecture taught, as some teach treatment below a count threshold.
